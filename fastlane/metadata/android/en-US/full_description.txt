Opensolr Keys is a keyboard that looks and feels like the iOS keyboard and does nothing else.

- No network permission. Nothing you type ever leaves the phone.
- No accounts, no analytics, no dependencies. One small app.
- Big, bordered keys with the iOS layout, a number row, key preview and long-press symbols and accents (Romanian diacritics first).
- Small, context-aware autocorrect from offline word tables for English and Romanian. Romanian diacritics are restored by context. Words you keep as typed are remembered, and the list is yours to edit.
- Emoji picker with search, plus emoji suggestions while typing.
- The Pixel tap sound with its own volume, vibration with its own strength, key size and bottom offset, long-press delay.
